Step into the story

Overview

Zava Outdoor and its Summit Packs business unit are invented for this workshop. Aster, Brook, and Cinder are fictional operating regions. Every workbook row, reporting period, and business situation below is fabricated.

Fictional practice company

Your role
You are the regional operations/finance analyst responsible for the Summit Packs quarterly business review.
Situation
Sales have grown, but shipping costs and returns are drawing questions. The leadership team has fragments of analysis rather than a traceable decision story.
Trigger
The fictional regional sponsor asks for a review that distinguishes measured change from untested explanations before deciding what evidence to collect next.
Deadline
Your practice review begins in 90 minutes. Each chapter adds a reviewed row to one QBR Story sheet rather than restarting the analysis.
Keep your work together: QBR Story sheet

Where: Summit-QBR.xlsx > QBR Story

Keep one StoryLog table with RowID, Claim, Evidence, Limitation, NextAction, and ReviewStatus. Add to it as you go, then use those checked rows to build your slides and test your skill.

Prepare your workspace

Setup

  1. Open a blank practice workbook and a blank PowerPoint deck. Use an approved environment with the relevant Copilot capabilities; names and availability vary.
  2. Ask your facilitator for the practice files and checkpoints before you start. Files marked Provided separately are not included with this page.
  3. Without a source workbook, paste the fictional table from the first step into Excel and name it QuarterData. Use only the practice data.
  4. Create a working copy named Summit-QBR.xlsx. Keep the original data unchanged and place calculations on separate sheets.
  5. Follow the cumulative agenda. Use checkpoints only to rejoin; inspect their starting state before continuing.

Choose the right mode

Chat
Ask questions, check formulas, and review the story before changing your files.
Plan
Agree on a sequence and output structure before making edits. If no separate Plan mode is available, ask Chat for a plan.
Edit
Ask for workbook or slide changes, then check the result. Paste the changes yourself when editing is unavailable.
Cowork
Turn your reviewed workflow into a personal skill, approve saving it, and reuse it on another quarter.

Ground rules

  • Use only the fictional practice data, not real customer workbooks or personal information.
  • A model's confidence is not evidence. Verify ranges, signs, denominators, and source references before carrying a claim forward.
  • Do not infer why shipping costs or returns changed from this small table alone.
  • Review proposed edits and check the resulting cells or slides. Use the manual-paste option when editing is unavailable.
  • Review your work before saving it, and keep it private during this practice session.

10 min / Basic

Inspect, reconcile, then write

Establish the source and verify weighted ratios before making a narrative claim.

Uses: Excel > fictional quarterly source, then QuarterData and QuarterSummary

Work in a new practice workbook. Treat the TSV as the whole evidence base: it contains no shipment detail, customer mix, or cause codes.

Plan the source check Plan 
Plan my Summit Packs QBR in Excel using only this table as QuarterData. Check duplicate Quarter/Region pairs, blanks, numeric types and positive denominators. NetSales and ShipCost are currency; ReturnUnits counts returned Units. Stick to the supplied rows and leave causes open. Show me the checks and output sheets before we edit.

Quarter	Region	Units	NetSales	ShipCost	ReturnUnits
FY28-Q1	Aster	800	72000	4320	32
FY28-Q1	Brook	600	54000	4320	36
FY28-Q1	Cinder	500	45000	3150	25
FY28-Q2	Aster	840	75600	5040	42
FY28-Q2	Brook	620	55800	5580	62
FY28-Q2	Cinder	540	48600	3780	27
Reconcile the baseline Chat 
Using the checked QuarterData, total Units, NetSales, ShipCost and ReturnUnits by quarter. Show formulas and ranges for shipping/sales and returns/units, dividing totals rather than averaging rates. Keep full precision; display percentages to four decimals. Flag missing or zero denominators and questions the data cannot answer.
Write only the checked summary Edit 
After my review, create QuarterSummary for FY28-Q1 and FY28-Q2 with formulas, units and a source-total check. Keep QuarterData unchanged. Label it Fictional practice; causes remain unknown. If editing fails, give me the formulas and cell layout to paste.

Wording varies; the substance should match

A traceable two-quarter summary with weighted rates and no invented explanations.

  • FY28-Q2: units 2,000; net sales 180,000; shipping 14,400; returned units 131.
  • FY28-Q1: units 1,900; net sales 171,000; shipping 11,790; returned units 93.
  • FY28-Q2 shipping burden is 8%; return rate is 6.55%. Source totals, not averages of regional rates, supply the denominators.
Need another way?

If Edit cannot write it, paste the TSV into Excel, format it as QuarterData, and manually insert the reviewed summary formulas. Verify the resulting cells before moving on.

Warning

Verify all amounts and denominator choices yourself. ReturnUnits is a count; mixing it with currency creates a meaningless ratio.

Tip

Keep the original table unchanged so later claims can always be traced back to the same evidence.

Carry forward: QuarterSummary is the checked input for the next step; its source and review limits will become StoryLog row baseline.

Build a movement view

Locate the largest shipping increase without confusing it with the sales-growth leader.

Uses: Excel > StoryLog baseline, QuarterSummary, and preserved QuarterData

Start with your checked baseline. Compare the regions and reconcile their changes before adding the finding to your Story sheet.

Explain the regional movement Chat 
Using StoryLog baseline and QuarterData, compare FY28-Q2 with FY28-Q1 by region: sales and shipping changes, FY28-Q2 return rates, and shares of the shipping increase. Show formulas and reconcile to QuarterSummary. Separate shipping from sales leaders; causes need more evidence. List questions to investigate.
Append the evidence-backed driver Edit 
After my review, save RegionalDrivers and add StoryLog regional-driver linked to baseline and checked cells. Keep all regions visible. Brook needs investigation, not blame: note missing shipment detail in Limitation and the proposed check in NextAction. If editing fails, give me both tables to paste.

Wording varies; the substance should match

A reconciled regional movement view and one limited, source-linked investigation claim.

  • Net sales increase by 9,000: Aster +3,600, Brook +1,800, Cinder +3,600.
  • Shipping increases by 2,610: Aster +720, Brook +1,260, Cinder +630.
  • Brook accounts for about 48.28% of the shipping increase and has a FY28-Q2 return rate of 10%; neither fact proves a cause.
Need another way?

If Edit cannot write RegionalDrivers, build a PivotTable or formula-based comparison manually, then paste the reviewed StoryLog row.

Warning

A largest movement is a prioritization signal, not causal evidence. Retain all three regions in the reconciliation.

Instructor

Ask a learner to explain the difference between contribution to growth and a regional rate before opening the challenge answer.

8 min / Challenge

Challenge a tempting headline

Correct the narrative independently before it reaches a slide.

Uses: Excel > RegionalDrivers and StoryLog regional-driver from Build a movement view

Pause Copilot. Inspect the checked tables yourself, then add a correction to the same Story sheet.

Your challenge - try without a prompt

A draft says: 'Brook generated all sales growth, and the unit's return rate is 10%, so returns caused the shipping increase.' Identify each unsupported or incorrect statement. Write a corrected StoryLog row claim-check with references and an explicit uncertainty.

Hint: Compare contribution to the sales movement, the scope of the return-rate denominator, and what the source says about causality.

Show answer

Brook contributes 1,800 of the 9,000 sales increase, or 20%, not all of it. The unit-wide FY28-Q2 return rate is 131/2,000 = 6.55%; 10% applies only to Brook. The table contains no evidence that returns caused shipping costs to rise. A defensible correction prioritizes Brook for investigation while preserving the other regions and labeling the cause unknown.

Wording varies; the substance should match

A correction that separates contribution, rate scope, and unsupported causality.

Need another way?

If formulas are hard to inspect, use the reviewed RegionalDrivers output and the source table side by side; ask the facilitator for navigation help, not the answer.

Important

Try the challenge before revealing the answer. Keep your corrected claim even if its wording differs from the answer.

Troubleshooting

A named mode is not available
Use Chat for a plan or explanation and carry out the reviewed changes manually.
Edit cannot write to the workbook or presentation
Ask for exact formulas, rows, or slide text. Paste them manually and verify the actual artifact before changing ReviewStatus.
The quarter ratios differ from expectations
Check the selected rows, numeric types, and denominators. Divide totals by totals; do not average regional percentages.
The chapter checkpoint is not supplied
Ask the facilitator or Cowork content-writing skill for the declared starting state. Files labeled Provided separately are not hidden download links.
The slides add an unsupported cause
Return to claim-check and decision-request. Replace the unsupported sentence and preserve the limitation in the slide notes.
The replay appears to pass suspiciously well
Check whether the answer key or prior outputs leaked into the run. Repeat with only the prior-quarter source and record the result honestly.
Personal skill creation is unavailable
Save summit-review-prompt.txt and paste it into Cowork for each review. Ask your facilitator about skill availability in your environment.
Facilitator: preparing the practice files
This sample lists files but does not bundle them. Prepare and check the workbooks and checkpoints before the session. The MCP server renders the guide only; saving files and skills happens in Cowork with the learner's approval. A local skill draft alone is not a saved Cowork skill.
